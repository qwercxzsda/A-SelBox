# Company fees

Each SKU’s current terms identify its owner and contain marketplace-specific rate periods. The source posting date in marketplace-local time selects the fee rate. Only the signed product price of a sale or refund is eligible. A refund therefore reduces the SelBox service fee. Shipping, tax, advertising and other costs contribute no fee base.

Changes to ownership apply to all activity dates. Saved reports pin their terms. A missing rate prevents publication for the affected company and month. See [payout reports](company_payout_reports.md).
