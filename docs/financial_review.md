# Financial review

The administrator’s Financial review covers one marketplace and one year in that marketplace’s currency. Every Finances amount becomes a Company amount, a SelBox type or a Difference, with base and tax kept apart. A Difference cannot reach a company yet. Its reason says why: no assignment, reference not found, rounding errors, totals differ, charged in another month, split ownership, ledger month not loaded, not in ledger, or no ID on the charge.

Reference report SKUs remain evidence. A missing ledger SKU assignment can be necessary even when that SKU has never appeared on a sale. A campaign, promotion or shipment can be assigned manually even when Amazon has no product list. The assignment screen shows product hints and warnings. Changing an assignment updates historical live calculations and queues eligible payouts for refresh.

## Database reads

Both review functions are administrator-only. Other accounts get SQLSTATE 42501, and invalid arguments get 22023.

- `financial_review_months(p_marketplace, p_year)` returns one row per month, currency and measure, summed across seller accounts and regions. `FINANCES` is the native control: every Finances activity amount. `COMPANY`, `SELBOX` (one row per SelBox group) and `DIFFERENCE` (one row per reason) are the outcomes. Each row has `base_amount`, `tax_amount` and `amount_count`. In every month, `FINANCES` equals `COMPANY` + `SELBOX` + `DIFFERENCE`, for base and for tax.
- `financial_outcome_page(p_outcome, p_month, p_marketplace, p_reason, p_selbox_group, p_limit, p_offset)` lists the `DIFFERENCE` or `SELBOX` records of one marketplace-month, newest first. A reason narrows differences and a group narrows SelBox types. A code outside `private.difference_reasons()` or `private.selbox_group` gets 22023 instead of an empty page. The filters apply before the page, so the row count equals `amount_count` for the same filters and the month drawer shows an exact total without a count query.
- `private.monthly_outcomes(p_from, p_until, p_marketplace)` keeps the seller account and region grain. Seed verification checks each account month against the native Finances amounts and each marketplace-year against `financial_review_months`.

## Screen

The scope is a Marketplace and a Year. Both use constant options: every marketplace (Amazon.com by default) and the years from 2024, the earliest Finances activity Amazon returns, to the current UTC year. Each marketplace has exactly one currency, so there is no currency choice. The client rejects any row in another currency, any reason or SelBox group it does not define, any record source it does not define, and any month whose Finances differ from Company + SelBox + Difference, for base or for tax. It shows a red alert with Retry and no totals.

- **Cards.** Finances, Company amounts, SelBox types and Differences for the year, each including tax with the tax beneath. Amounts keep every digit, padded to the currency's minor units.
- **Differences by month.** Each month's differences stack up (positive) or down (negative) by reason, one fixed color per reason. One line shows the running difference from January. Months without activity are labelled gaps. The legend lists each reason's year total, largest first. A year without differences shows `No differences in <year>` in a card of the same height. The chart is empty only when every month's every reason is zero; a reason whose months cancel over the year still shows its bars, with a 0.00 year total in the legend. The legend reserves the rows of three reasons at every width, so an empty year and a typical one share the card height; a year with more reasons makes the card taller.
- **Months.** One row per month with activity: Finances, Company amounts, SelBox types, Differences and the running difference.
- **Month drawer.** A month, a Months row or a reason segment opens it; a segment also selects its reason. It shows the month's totals, the differences by reason with their records, the SelBox types by group (collapsed) and payout readiness (collapsed). Selecting a reason or group narrows the records, 50 per page with the exact total from `amount_count`. Another month, reason or group starts its records on page 1, also a month seen before or one opened again; another month also starts payout readiness on page 1. Finances records, and the balancing entries computed from a Finances charge (source KEY: rounding errors, totals differ, charged in another month), show the source "Finances" and open that charge's source record. An opened record belongs to the records list that opened it and closes with that month, reason or group, also by Back or a link. "Previous month" and "Next month" in the drawer header move between the year's months with activity. Payout readiness lists one row per company (Company, Status, Note, Next retry (UTC)); a company missing fee rates expands to its SKUs and marketplaces. Selecting a reason or group with fewer records keeps the drawer where it is.

Marketplace, year, the open month (`row`), reason and group are in the URL. A link whose month has no activity, or whose reason or group the month lacks, drops those values in place of the link's history entry, so Back returns to the page before it; a linked group opens the SelBox types section.
