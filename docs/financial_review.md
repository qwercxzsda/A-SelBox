# Financial review

The administrator’s Financial review shows Finances activity by company, SelBox and difference, with base and tax in the charge currency. A missing report, missing assignment, split ownership, absent ID, ledger gap, rounding or mismatch identifies what prevents the charge from reaching a company.

Reference report SKUs remain evidence. A missing ledger SKU assignment can be necessary even when that SKU has never appeared on a sale. A campaign, promotion or shipment can be assigned manually even when Amazon has no product list. The assignment screen shows product hints and warnings. Changing an assignment updates historical live calculations and queues eligible payouts for refresh.

See [source matching](source_allocation.md) for the accounting rules.
